# Visitor safety and public identity

The header's Quick exit link opens the US National Weather Service in the current tab. Ordinary activation with JavaScript replaces the current history entry. With scripts disabled the browser follows the link normally and Back can revisit the page. Earlier visits, same-page navigation history, device monitoring, network records, downloaded files, receipts, and payment records can persist. The page explains the limitations in the FAQ and footer.

The document uses a no-referrer policy and the exit link uses noreferrer. The exit handler only navigates. Keep analytics listeners, tracking parameters, storage, and confirmation dialogs off this action. Fonts and editorial images load from the landing page's own host. Gumroad receives purchase details during its hosted checkout.

Public copy uses the publisher and approved role-level description. Obtain owner approval before adding biographical details. Inspect the seller profile and actual receipt reply-to identity before launch. Readers can contact the seller by replying to a receipt. Do not ask readers to submit relationship histories through the landing page.
