Common Patterns

Control is not always loud.

Sometimes the first sign is not certainty. It is the strain of replaying conversations, coercive control often shows up in small, everyday patterns.

  • Conversations that leave people unsure what was meant.
  • Boundaries met with guilt or withdrawal.
  • Adjusting everyday needs to avoid conflict.
  • Coercive control is widely documented, and many people have experienced it.
