# Paste into Systeme.io

Use the files in numerical order.

1. In your **sales-page** editor, open **Settings** and paste the entire contents of `01-header-code.html` into the header-code field.
2. Drag an **HTML raw-code** element into the page. Choose **Edit code** and paste the entire contents of `02-page-body.html`.
3. In **Settings**, paste the entire contents of `03-footer-code.html` into the footer-code field.
4. In `02-page-body.html`, replace these two placeholders before pasting:
   - `YOUR_PUBLIC_PROBLEM_IMAGE_URL` — the public URL of the journal image.
   - `YOUR_PUBLIC_SOLUTION_IMAGE_URL` — the public URL of the final solution image, once available.
5. Replace every occurrence of `YOUR_SYSTEME_CHECKOUT_URL` with your completed Systeme.io checkout-step URL.
6. Save and check the published page on a real mobile device. Systeme.io does not render raw HTML reliably inside its editor preview.

Do not add `<html>`, `<head>`, `<body>`, or `<footer>` wrappers: these blocks are already formatted for their respective Systeme.io fields.

The hero visual is intentionally a simple placeholder. Replace it only after you have selected the final hero image.
